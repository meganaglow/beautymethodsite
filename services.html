Treatments

Selected with intention.

You do not need to arrive knowing what treatment you need. We assess the whole picture first, then recommend the treatment — or combination of treatments — that makes sense for you.

01 · Neuromodulators

Botox & Dysport

Natural movement. A softer expression.

Botox and Dysport temporarily relax the muscle activity behind expression lines. Dosing and placement are mapped to your anatomy and how your face moves — never a predetermined pattern.

Common areas
Forehead, frown lines, crow's feet
Appointment
≈ 30 minutes
Results
Fully visible around two weeks
Duration
≈ 3–4 months
Downtime
Minimal; small bumps or bruising possible
Suitability, dosing, contraindications, risks, and expected results are reviewed during a medical consultation.

03 · Collagen induction

Microneedling

Invest in the quality of your skin.

Microneedling creates controlled microchannels that trigger the skin's own healing response, building collagen and elastin from within.

Over a series it supports smoother, brighter skin — refining pores, improving texture, and softening fine lines. It also supports the skin's healing response in acne, rosacea, and mild pigmentation, with overall tissue remodeling as the goal.

Appointment
≈ 60 minutes, incl. topical numbing
Device
SkinPen Elite
Results
Build as collagen remodels
Downtime
24–72 hours of pink skin

04 · Intense pulsed light

IPL Photofacial

Clearer, more even-toned skin.

IPL uses filtered light to target the reds and browns in the skin — sun damage, uneven pigment, and visible redness — for a clearer, more even complexion.

Our Candela Nordlys uses Selective Waveband Technology, which filters the light to the wavebands suited to each concern, including hyperpigmentation and vascular redness such as rosacea.

Treats
Sun damage, brown spots and hyperpigmentation, redness and rosacea
Device
Candela Nordlys, SWT IPL
Appointment
≈ 45 minutes; numbing used as needed
Series
Typically 3 sessions; varies with your concerns and goals
Results
Clearer skin with more even color and tone, and a brighter appearance
Downtime
None. Brown spots darken after treatment and typically clear in 4–7 days
Suitability, contraindications, risks, and expected results are reviewed during a medical consultation.

05 · Non-ablative fractional laser

Laser Resurfacing

Renew texture, tone, and firmness.

Fractional lasers treat a small fraction of the skin at a time — fine columns of energy surrounded by untouched skin — prompting renewal while the surrounding tissue supports healing. The Nordlys offers two resurfacing options, chosen to match your goals.

Frax 1550

Deeper remodeling

Reaches deeper into the skin to stimulate collagen remodeling for anti-aging — softening fine lines and wrinkles and refining texture.

Frax 1940

Surface renewal

Works closer to the surface to brighten, refine texture and fine lines, and improve superficial pigment and discoloration.

Device
Candela Nordlys, Frax 1550 & Frax 1940
Appointment
≈ 1 hour, incl. topical numbing
Series
Typically 3 sessions; varies with your concerns and goals
Results
Smoother, more refined texture and a firmer feel; best results about 3 months after treatment, once new collagen has built
Downtime
2–6 days, depending on treatment. Redness, swelling, and a sandpaper-like texture are expected while skin heals
Suitability, contraindications, risks, and expected results are reviewed during a medical consultation.
