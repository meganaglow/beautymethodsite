About

A more personal kind of practice.

The Beauty Method is an independently owned aesthetic practice in Aptos, created around a shared belief that exceptional aesthetic medicine should combine clinical judgment with highly personal care.

Medical Director

Nik Greenson, MD, FACEP

An emergency physician with a passion for non-surgical facial aesthetic medicine.

Dr. Greenson earned his medical degree from the University of California, San Diego in 2001 and completed his residency training in emergency medicine at Stanford in 2004.

Dr. Nik is known for his meticulous, safety-first approach, and prioritizes comprehensive patient education and long-term, natural, healthy results. Outside the clinic he practices pilates, sails, climbs, dives, and is raising his two teenage sons.

  • Credentials MD, FACEP
  • Focus Non-surgical facial aesthetics
  • Training UC San Diego School of Medicine, 2001 · Emergency medicine residency, Stanford, 2004

Aesthetic Nurse Injector

Jillian Osland Matejcek, RN

A Registered Nurse in her thirteenth year of practice as a full-time aesthetic nurse injector.

Jillian spent seven years working exclusively in dermatology practices and another six in Bay Area medical spas. Having worked with a wide range of laser platforms and skin rejuvenation modalities, she brings deep experience across skin rejuvenation, lasers, injectables, and medical-grade skincare.

She is one of roughly 700 Registered Nurses in the country to hold the CANS (Certified Aesthetic Nurse Specialist) designation. With close attention to detail and a belief that less is more, safety and natural results drive her approach to every patient outcome.

Jillian has lived in Santa Cruz County her entire life and spends her free time with her loved ones and her daughter.

  • Credentials RN · CANS (Certified Aesthetic Nurse Specialist)
  • Role Aesthetic Nurse Injector
  • Experience 13 years in aesthetic practice
